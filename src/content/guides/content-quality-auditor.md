# Content Quality Auditor

Audit documents, pages, or content libraries with a general quality rubric or a specialized blog rubric, then prioritize improvements.

## Available Modes

For blog posts, newsletters, or a thought-leadership library, use the blog mode. It retains the original blog dimensions, editorial action labels, and single-post or library backlog output. Other content uses the general rubric unless you supply your own.
