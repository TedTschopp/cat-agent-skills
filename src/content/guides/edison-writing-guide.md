# Edison Writing Guide

Draft or edit news articles, briefs, headlines, press releases, and media
advisories with the supplied newsroom style guide. The skill applies AP style,
leads with the news, and checks attribution, names, titles, numbers, dates,
times, and paragraph length.

## What to Provide

Provide the draft or source notes, the intended audience, the publication, and
any house-style exceptions. Include verified names, quotations, dates, and
source details needed for the piece.

## Example Requests

- "Edit this brief using the Edison Writing Guide. Keep every verified fact
  and quotation intact."
- "Turn these interview notes into a news article with a concise lede and
  clear attribution."
- "Review this headline and opening paragraph for AP style."

## Included Files

- `SKILL.md`: the agent's drafting and editing instructions.
- `references/example-transformations.md`: before-and-after examples.

The downloadable ZIP contains the skill and its reference file. This guide and
the catalog metadata stay outside the download.

## House Style

The supplied guide asks the agent to confirm house style before writing for a
new publication. Provide those exceptions with the source material.
