# Office Rubrics

Choose a family after verifying current official duties. These are proposed defaults, not legal eligibility tests or official professional ratings. Adapt for the actual office, explain why, and fix the rubric before candidate scoring. Each contest's weights total 100.

The education-executive rubric preserves the six weights recovered from the prior superintendent analysis. The other numerical weights are new defaults; the earlier judge, sheriff, and assessor assessments were qualitative. Score 0–4 using `research-and-scoring.md`; supply criterion-specific 0, 2, and 4 anchors in the report data. Missing ratings are null, not zero. Ideological agreement does not earn qualification points.

## Executive Offices

Examples: president and vice president, governor, mayor, county executive. Assess running mates' roles where applicable. Transferable leadership counts; the same prior title is not required.

| Criterion | Weight | Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Relevant leadership and scope | 25 | Responsibility for institutions, personnel, crises, and decisions at relevant scale |
| Demonstrated results | 25 | Delivery and failures, context, and the candidate's actual responsibility |
| Governing, budgets, and implementation | 20 | Resource allocation, execution, oversight, and institutional limits |
| Integrity and judgment | 20 | Verified conduct, accountability, conflicts, and decision quality |
| Institutional and intergovernmental readiness | 10 | Understanding powers, constraints, and required working relationships |

## Legislative and Governing Boards

Examples: Congress, legislatures, councils, school boards, special-district boards. Members govern collectively; do not score them as personally managing all operations.

| Criterion | Weight | Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Lawmaking, governance, and oversight | 25 | Relevant decision-making, scrutiny, and collective governance |
| Demonstrated public results | 20 | Outcomes and actual contribution, including transferable community work |
| Budget and policy competence | 20 | Public finances, tradeoffs, evidence, and subject-matter understanding |
| Integrity and judgment | 20 | Verified ethics, accountability, conflicts, and decision quality |
| Constituent service and implementation relationships | 15 | Responsiveness and ability to translate decisions into service |

For school or specialized boards, make their subject-specific duties explicit in the anchors. Do not reward promises to exercise powers an individual member does not possess.

## Judicial Elections and Retention

| Criterion | Weight | Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Independent professional assessment | 30 | Current relevant bar or judicial-performance evaluations and their methods and limits |
| Temperament, integrity, and impartial administration | 25 | Verified conduct, official findings, fairness, and courtroom temperament |
| Legal and adjudicative competence | 25 | Relevant practice, decisions, hearings, trials, commissioner or judicial work; quality as well as volume |
| Breadth and fit for the court | 15 | Relevant criminal, civil, family, juvenile, administrative, or appellate experience |
| Leadership and professional contribution | 5 | Supervision, training, court improvement, and relevant teaching |

Use comparable assessment programs for the jurisdiction. If no program exists for any candidate, adapt before scoring and disclose the reallocation. Do not invent ratings or change weights to excuse an individual's absent evaluation. A verified temperament concern can outweigh a favorable rating. Pending allegations are not findings. Retention compares a record to a standard, not a fictional rival.

## Education Executives

The recovered rubric was for California State Superintendent of Public Instruction. Verify whether the supplied office is statewide, countywide, or district-level and adapt its duties accordingly.

| Criterion | Weight | Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Education governance and executive readiness | 25 | School or system governance, administration, fiscal and compliance readiness |
| Policy and budget capability | 20 | This office's funding, policy, and intergovernmental responsibilities |
| Student-outcomes agenda | 20 | Specificity, evidence, feasibility, accountability, and authority; agreement is scored separately |
| Coalition and implementation capacity | 15 | Demonstrated delivery through educator, family, community, and governmental relationships |
| Jurisdiction and system relevance | 10 | Relevant scale and population served, including transferable urban or rural experience |
| Classroom and educator credibility | 10 | Teaching, school-site, or educator-preparation experience |

Endorsements can document relationships; their count and popularity do not independently earn points. Do not use birthplace or personal identity as a proxy for jurisdictional relevance.

## Other Offices and Measures

For an unfamiliar office, map official duties to observable criteria. Give its technical duties meaningful weight while preserving performance, integrity, and governing or administrative readiness. Publish weights and rationale before interpreting candidate evidence.

Measures have no candidate-qualification score. Analyze the current rule, change, Yes/No effects, fiscal assumptions, cost and benefit distribution, implementation, oversight, discretion, expiration, and uncertainty. Assess alignment only against explicit priorities. Keep judicial retention distinct from competing races and policy measures.
